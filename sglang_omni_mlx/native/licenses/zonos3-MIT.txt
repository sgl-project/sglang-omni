The ZONOS3-Mini backbone, sampling, text normalization, codec decoder and
speaker encoder are adapted from Blaizzy/mlx-audio at commit
2c4122d7521cb228f6c64675ad411632f8bb6102 (mlx_audio/tts/models/zonos3 and
mlx_audio/tts/models/zonos2/textnorm.py),
which adapts them from Zyphra's reference implementation
(github.com/Zyphra/ZONOS3-Mini at 6ff97cac0c684732de5f2bc6e3671aa783c15054).
The gated delta Metal kernel is mlx-lm's (mlx_lm/models/gated_delta.py), as
mlx-audio vendors it. All three are MIT-licensed.
