# Changelog

All notable changes to Voxt. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Each version's section is its release notes: English first, then the same in Chinese under `### 中文`, with `####` headings below it (docs/release.md §2.1).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Translate goes both ways between your main language and the target, and takes follow-ups** (#3897): when the text you select or say is already in the target language, Translate writes it in your main language instead. With Chinese as your main language and English as the target, Chinese goes into English and English into Chinese, with no change of setting. Voxt tells the two apart by their script, so it does this only for languages written differently (English and French keep the target). A language you pick on the answer card stays as picked. With text selected, Translate still translates the selection and does not record. The translation's answer card now has Continue, as Rewrite's does: press it (or the Rewrite shortcut) and say what to change, such as "make it shorter", and the language model changes that translation in the card.
- **Upload size and recording length limits in Settings → Audio** (#3840): Upload size limit sets the largest piece Voxt sends to OpenAI or Doubao file recognition (0, the default, keeps the service's own: 25 MB and 100 MB). Recording length limit stops a recording at that many minutes and recognises it (0, the default, means no limit). Both apply to Transcribe, Translate and Rewrite. While you record, the recording window warns in the last minute before the length limit, and from 30 seconds before a recording passes the upload size and goes in pieces.
- **Transcribe a recording from the command line** (#3845): `Voxt transcribe meeting.m4a -o meeting.srt` turns an audio file, or the sound of an mp4 or mov video, into SRT subtitles (or plain text with `--format txt`) with Transcribe's speech model, while Voxt runs. It splits the recording at pauses as meetings do, writes the file only when it finishes, and exits with a clear message when the file cannot be read or the model fails. Nothing goes into History. See docs/transcribe.md.
- **Send a screenshot to language models that read images** (#3846): turn on Settings → Privacy → Send the window to the language model, and when you start Transcribe, Translate or Rewrite, Voxt takes a screenshot of the window you speak into and sends it to the language model with the text, so names and terms on screen come out right. Off by default. It needs Screen Recording permission, which Voxt asks for when you turn it on. A model that takes no images gets the text alone, as before.
- **Rewrite can send the image you copied** (#3846): turn on Settings → Privacy → Send the copied image with Rewrite, copy an image (⌘C in Preview, a browser, a chat app, or an image file in Finder), then rewrite. Voxt sends that image to the language model with the selected text and what you said, so you can ask about it or describe it. Off by default. Copied text, and copied files that are not images, are not sent. A model that takes no images gets the text alone. When both settings are on, the copied image goes instead of the screenshot.
- **Rewrite: Continue adds the text you select now**: select more text (say, the messages that came in since) before you press Continue or the Rewrite shortcut in the answer card, and the follow-up sends it to the language model with your new instruction, next to the text selected the first time. The card shows it under what you said, shortened until you click Show all. With nothing new selected, the follow-up works as before.
- **Personal dictionary** on the Dictionary page: write your names, terms and numbers once in Markdown, for example a table of the standard spelling, what speech recognition writes instead, and when it applies. Transcribe, Translate and Rewrite add it to the language model's prompt, right after the task's rules (also with your own full prompt), and speech recognition does not get it. The model gets your notes exactly as you wrote them, with no fixed text of Voxt's around them, so a rule you want goes in the notes or in your own prompt. How it is used, under the editor, shows an example built from your settings: the system prompt, the dictionary, your input and the speech recognition text, then what the model receives. The switch Add to the language model prompt keeps it out; terms and aliases work as before.
- **Stop an answer part way** (#3886): while a Rewrite answer streams into the answer card, click Stop in the card or press the cancel shortcut (Esc unless you changed it). Voxt ends the request to the language model, and the card keeps the text written so far, to copy, insert or follow up on. A follow-up's answer stops the same way. Before the first words, or with the answer card off, the cancel shortcut still cancels the whole session.

- **Pick the thinking level for one input in the answer card** (#3879): when the language model service that answered takes a thinking setting, the Translate and Rewrite answer card has a button before Insert. It lists the levels the service offers (As in settings, off, on, its effort levels, or a budget of 1,024, 4,096 or 16,384 tokens). Pick one and Voxt asks again with the same input at that level and replaces the answer in the card. The service's Thinking setting does not change, and the next input uses it again.

It names the level in use, for example "Thinking: Off", and lists the same levels as the answer card. Pick one and Voxt stops the request and cleans up the same words again at that level. Only that answer is inserted. The service's Thinking setting does not change, and the next input uses it again.
- **Thinking off for short inputs, and the thinking button says it asks again** (#3951): a language model service set to think (on, an effort level or a budget) has a new **Off for short inputs** switch under Thinking. Inputs with fewer characters than its limit (200 by default) go out with Voxt's default thinking, which turns thinking off where the service lets it, so short sentences come back without the wait. Longer inputs use the chosen level. The thinking button on the answer card and in the recording window now reads "Ask again: Thinking…" or, for example, "Ask again: Thinking (Off)", because picking a level stops the answer and asks again.

### Changed

- **Wait for the language model takes your own number of minutes** (#3941): besides Automatic, 1, 2, 5 and 10 minutes, the setting on the Language models page has Custom…, which shows a minutes field. Type or step any whole number from 1 to 60, and Voxt waits at least that long for Transcribe, Translate and Rewrite, as with the presets. A number outside 1 to 60 is not saved. Settings saved before keep their wait.
- **Faster transcription is a switch** (#3873): on a speech model's card in Speech models, Faster transcription now has a switch, and its ⓘ says it needs a draft model and how large it is. The switch decides whether the model uses the draft. Downloading the draft no longer turns it on, and turning it off keeps the draft on disk without using it. With the switch on and no draft, the card asks you to download it. If you downloaded the draft in 2.0.2 or 2.0.3, the switch starts on.
- **The speech model loads before the first dictation**: Voxt now loads Transcribe's on-device speech model when it starts and when you choose another one, instead of at the first press of the shortcut. The first live text after a launch or a change of model no longer waits for the load (0.2 to 0.5 s on an M-series Mac with 36 GB, longer on a busy Mac or one with less memory). The model leaves memory after 10 idle minutes, as before.
- **A large dictionary no longer slows dictation down**: after you dictate, Voxt replaces the dictionary's aliases with their terms. It now checks only the aliases that start with the letter at each place. With 1,000 terms, a 4 KB transcript took 57 ms and now takes 2 ms; a 20 KB one took 280 ms and now takes 8 ms (Apple M5 Max). The result is the same.
- **Translating selected text no longer waits for the speech model** (#3838): Translate with text selected uses only the language model (and its fallback), so Voxt no longer opens the on-device speech model first. After 10 idle minutes that model leaves memory, and loading it again made a selection translation wait for a model it never used. It also works with no speech model installed. With nothing selected, Translate records and uses the speech model as before.
- **Faster transcription uses less memory**: the speech model's draft now runs at 8 bits instead of 16. On Voxt's test recordings the text is the same word for word, the speech model uses about 120 MB (0.6B) or 300 MB (1.7B) less memory, and recognition is 1–2% faster (Apple M5 Max).
- **Long History records scroll smoothly**: with the details of a record over about 57,000 characters open (an hour of dictation, a long article you translated), every frame laid its text out again, so the whole History page reacted slowly: a tenth of a second per mouse move or scroll at 60,000 characters, almost 4 s at 100,000. Voxt now lays the text out once when the details open; each frame after that takes 0.3 ms (Apple M5 Max). Shorter records were fast already.

### Fixed

- **Set up a language model works on the setup guide's Translate and Rewrite steps** (#3917): the button opened the Language models page behind the guide, so it seemed to do nothing. The guide now steps aside, as the same button on the Models step does, and the page offers Back to the setup guide at that step.
- **Voxt stays out of the Dock from launch** (#3918): with Settings → General → Show in the Dock off, Voxt started as an app in the Dock and left it only once it had finished launching, and its Dock icon could stay, for example after ⌘W closed the window. Voxt now starts out of the Dock, with only its menu bar icon.
- **Rewrite reads text selected on a web page or in a PDF** (#3835): with a whole page selected with ⌘A in Chrome, or a page of a PDF selected in Acrobat, Rewrite sent the language model only your instruction, and the model answered that no text was given. Voxt took the empty selection that these apps report to Accessibility for no selection at all and did not try copying. It now copies the selection there (and puts your clipboard back), as it already did in apps that report nothing. In a text field, a caret with nothing selected still sends no text.
- **No more Voxt 1.x announcements, and none in another language**: Voxt reads the announcements of Voxt 1.x's server, which still lists its 1.13 announcement of meetings and notes, in Chinese. Every new Voxt 2 user saw it as a card in the sidebar, in the English UI too, although Voxt 2's release builds have no meetings or notes. Voxt now shows only announcements for Voxt 2, and Chinese ones only in the Chinese UI. If the card is still there, dismiss it: it does not come back.
- **The answer card no longer jumps while a fast model writes a long answer** (#3836): when the language model wrote quickly (a fast remote model writing a long Rewrite answer), the card grew by a few lines on every chunk, several times a second. The card now shows the answer at a steady pace, a little behind the model, and grows about four lines at a time. The whole answer shows as soon as the model finishes, without waiting for the pace to catch up. A follow-up's answer streams the same way.
- **Beta Updates now finds betas** (#3850): with Settings → Diagnostics & About → Beta Updates on, Check for Updates… and the daily check offer the newest beta, built from main after each merged change, with its notes and a Download button for its disk image. You no longer need a GitHub login to get a beta. With Beta Updates off, you see only releases, as before. Voxt still does not install updates itself.
- **Model cards on Speech models line up** (#3854): a model in a card of its own (Parakeet, SenseVoice, Cohere, System dictation, ...) had its icon in a column of its own, so its name, description, languages and labels started further right than those of the Whisper and Qwen3-ASR family cards, with an empty strip under the icon. The icon now sits beside the model's name, and the text starts at the card's edge, as in a family card.
- **The answer card stays shut when its switch is off**: when a result could not go in (no text field had the focus, a password field, or the paste did not show), Transcribe and Translate opened the answer card even with their answer card turned off. Now the card opens only when it is on; otherwise Voxt copies the result and the overlay says to press ⌘V. The result is in History either way. Rewrite still keeps such an answer in the card, where you can ask a follow-up.
- **Long recordings work with OpenAI and Doubao file recognition** (#3840): OpenAI and the services that use its API took at most 25 MB of audio, about 13 minutes, and Doubao's fast file recognition at most 100 MB, about 52 minutes. A longer dictation, or Retry from History, failed with "over the 25 MB limit". Voxt now sends a longer recording in pieces under the limit, cut at a pause, and joins the text.
- **A dictation is no longer cut off at 10 minutes**: Transcribe, Translate and Rewrite stopped recording by themselves after 10 minutes, so a long dictation such as a podcast ended without a key press. A recording now has no time limit: it runs until you stop it.
- **Meetings use your main language**: the live transcript and the transcript made after a meeting now give the speech model the same language hint as dictation (your main language in Settings → General, such as Simplified or Traditional Chinese). Before, meetings gave no hint and the model guessed the language. The speech model still picks the Chinese script itself: Voxt does not convert between Simplified and Traditional.
- **Dictation goes into Raycast and other launcher panels**: a panel that takes the keyboard without bringing its app to the front (Raycast, Tinycast) got nothing and Voxt showed the answer card instead. The text is now pasted into the panel's field.
- **Meeting live text no longer repeats a sentence after a pause** (development builds): when the model changed the last words of a sentence after Voxt had shown it, the next live line started the whole sentence again. Now it shows only the words after the part that stayed the same. The transcript made after the meeting was not affected.
- **System dictation keeps what was said before a pause**: some macOS versions restart their speech recognition after a short pause, and the result held only the words after it, in History and in the app the text went to. Voxt now keeps each part and joins them.
- **One Escape closes a dialog** (History settings, the setup guide, Network, a language model's settings): with the keyboard focus on a button that shows a tip, such as the dialog's ×, the first Escape only hid the tip and the second closed the dialog.
- **Logs says when the filter matches nothing**: a filter that matches no line shows "Nothing matches" with Clear filter, on the Logs page and in the logs panel, instead of "No log lines yet."
- **Language model debug closes with Esc or ×**: opened from Try prompts on a model…, it had no close control: only the sidebar, or Open Language models before a model is set up, led away from it. Esc and the × at the top right now go back to Language models; Esc in an open menu still closes only the menu.
- **A dictation survives a speech model that quit**: when the on-device speech model's process died during a recording (a crash, or macOS ending it under memory pressure), stopping gave only the live text heard until then, with a warning. Voxt now starts the model again and recognises the whole recording, about 0.2 s later on an M5 Max.
- **The overlay preview keeps the pill on the chosen side** (Settings → Overlay style): at a large distance from the screen edge, Top centre drew the pill near the bottom of the preview and Bottom centre near the top. The pill now moves toward the middle as the distance grows and stays in its own half.
- **Show in the Dock** (Settings → General → Menu bar and login): Voxt 2.0.0 and 2.0.1 did not read Voxt 1.x's Show in Dock, so who set up Voxt 2 with Show in Dock off in Voxt 1.x stayed in the Dock. Unless Voxt 2.0.2 or later imported Voxt 1.x's data, or the switch is already off, Voxt reads it at the next start, once; turned back on, the Dock stays. Find in settings finds a setting by its name in any section (before, "Dock" or "clipboard" found nothing in General), and in Chinese by its English name too ("Dock" finds 在程序坞中显示). Turning the switch off keeps the Voxt window in front.
- **A connection test without an API key says why in words**: in a language model's dialog, Test connection with no key saved or typed now says "Paste the service's API key above, then test again." The ⓘ beside it now gives that whole sentence before the error's detail. Before, it showed only the error code (`llm.no_key provider="anthropic"`), and the message pointed you to the Language models page you were already on.
- **The command palette no longer offers Meetings in a release build.** Meetings is only in development builds, and choosing it in the palette opened a "Not found" page.
- **A crash leaves something to send**: when Voxt crashes in its own code, what it was doing is now in `~/Library/Logs/VoxtNew/crash.log`, which the next start renames `crash.log.1` and names in the log. Before, such a crash left no report (macOS writes none for it), so there was nothing to send. Save a diagnostic bundle (Settings → About) takes both files.

### 中文

#### 新增

- **翻译在主要语言和目标语言之间双向翻译，还能追问**（#3897）：选中或说出的文字已经是目标语言时，翻译改译成你的主要语言。主要语言是中文、目标是英语时，中文译成英文，英文译成中文，不用改设置。Voxt 按文字的书写系统区分两种语言，所以只对书写系统不同的语言这样做（英语和法语之间照旧译成目标语言）。在答案卡片上选的语言照选的来。选中了文字时，翻译仍然只译选区，不录音。翻译的答案卡片多了“继续”，和改写一样：点它（或按改写快捷键）说要怎么改，比如“再短一点”，语言模型就在卡片里改这段译文。
- **“设置 → 音频”里可以设上传大小上限和录音时长上限**（#3840）：上传大小上限决定 Voxt 每次发给 OpenAI 或豆包极速版的录音最大多大（默认 0，用服务自己的 25 MB 和 100 MB）。录音时长上限让录音到这么多分钟时自动停下并识别（默认 0，不限时长）。两项对转录、翻译和改写都生效。录音时，离时长上限还剩一分钟，录音窗口会提醒；录音还差 30 秒超过上传大小、要分段发送时，也会提醒。
- **在命令行里转录录音**（#3845）：Voxt 运行时，`Voxt transcribe meeting.m4a -o meeting.srt` 用转录功能的语音识别模型，把音频文件或 mp4、mov 视频的声音转成 SRT 字幕（`--format txt` 输出纯文本）。它像会议一样按停顿切段，转完才写文件；文件读不了或模型出错时说明原因并以非零状态退出。不会写进历史。用法见 docs/transcribe.md。
- **给能看图的语言模型发截图**（#3846）：在“设置 → 隐私”打开“把窗口截图发给语言模型”后，每次开始转录、翻译或改写时，Voxt 会截取你正在输入的窗口，和文字一起发给语言模型，让屏幕上的名字和术语写对。默认关闭。需要“屏幕录制”权限，打开时 Voxt 会请求。不能看图的模型照旧只收到文字。
- **改写可以带上你复制的图片**（#3846）：在“设置 → 隐私”打开“改写时发送复制的图片”，复制一张图片（在预览、浏览器、聊天软件里按 ⌘C，或在访达里复制图片文件），再用改写。Voxt 会把这张图片和选中的文字、你说的话一起发给语言模型，可以让它描述图片或回答关于图片的问题。默认关闭。复制的是文字，或者复制的文件不是图片时，不会发送。不能看图的模型只收到文字。两个开关都打开时，发复制的图片，不发截图。
- **改写：“继续”会带上现在选中的文字**：在答案卡片里点“继续”或按改写快捷键之前，先选中更多文字（比如之后新来的消息），追问时这段文字会和新的指令一起发给语言模型，和第一次选中的文字都在对话里。卡片在你说的话下面显示这段文字，长的先截短，点“展开”看全文。没有新选中文字时，追问和以前一样。
- **个人词典**（词典页）：用 Markdown 把名字、术语和数字的写法写一次，例如一张表：标准写法、语音识别会写成什么、什么情况下适用。转录、翻译和改写会把它加到语言模型的提示词里，紧跟在任务规则后面（用自己的完整提示词时也加），语音识别不会收到。模型收到的就是你写的内容，Voxt 不在前后另加固定的说明文字，想要的规则写在词典或自己的提示词里。编辑框下面的“怎么使用”用你的设置举例：系统提示词、个人词典、你的输入、语音识别文本，以及模型最后收到的内容。关掉“加到语言模型的提示词里”就不发送；词条和别名照旧生效。
- **回答可以停在半路**（#3886）：改写的回答写进答案卡片时，点卡片上的“停止”或按取消键（默认 Esc），Voxt 结束对语言模型的请求，卡片保留已写出的文字，可以复制、插入或继续追问。追问的回答也能这样停。回答还没写出字、或答案卡片关着时，取消键照旧取消整段。
- **个人词典**（词典页）：用 Markdown 把名字、术语和数字的写法写一次，例如一张表：标准写法、语音识别会写成什么、什么情况下适用。转录、翻译和改写会把它加到语言模型的提示词里，紧跟在任务规则后面（用自己的完整提示词时也加），语音识别不会收到。模型收到的就是你写的内容，Voxt 不在前后另加固定的说明文字或标签，想要的规则写在词典或自己的提示词里。编辑框下面的“怎么使用”用你的设置举例：系统提示词、个人词典、你的输入、语音识别文本，以及模型最后收到的内容。关掉“加到语言模型的提示词里”就不发送；词条和别名照旧生效。
- **语言模型处理时可以直接用识别原文，也可以让它多等一会**：转录或翻译在等语言模型时，浮窗显示“用识别原文”，点它或按取消键（默认 Esc），马上插入识别出的原文。语音翻译超时后插入识别原文并提示，和转录一样。语言模型页的“等待语言模型”设置转录、翻译、改写至少等多久（自动、1、2、5、10 分钟）。
- **语言模型处理时可以直接用识别原文，也可以让它多等一会**：转录或翻译在等语言模型时，浮窗显示“用识别原文”，点它或按取消键（默认 Esc），马上插入识别出的原文。语音翻译超时后插入识别原文并提示，和转录一样。语言模型页最上面、服务卡片之前的“等待语言模型”（不用往下滚就能看到，#3821）设置转录、翻译、改写至少等多久（自动、1、2、5、10 分钟）。

- **快捷键严格模式**（设置 → 快捷键，在快捷键表格上方，不用滚动就能看到，#3916）：录音时只有开始录音的那个快捷键能停止它，按别的功能的快捷键不起作用。默认关闭，和 Voxt 1.x 一样，任何功能的快捷键都能停止录音（#3823）。
- **搜索标出每个匹配，按回车跳到下一个**（词典页、历史页和“在提示词中查找”，#3869 的一部分）：输入的内容每处匹配都用红色粗体标出，当前那处是红底白字。按回车跳到下一处，Shift+回车跳到上一处，列表滚到那里，搜索框旁显示“第 2 处，共 5 处”这样的计数。词典在词条、别名和备注里标出匹配。历史的一行显示这条记录里含搜索词的那一行。“在提示词中查找”的匹配从主题色改成红色。
- **会议、设置和日志的搜索也标出匹配，按回车跳转**（#3869），和词典、历史一样。按转写内容找到的会议，行里显示转写中含搜索词的那句。“在设置中查找”在显示出来的设置名里标出匹配。日志的过滤框在每一行里标出匹配。
- **历史页加了“全部”标签**（#3848）：在转录、翻译、改写之后，“全部”把所有功能的记录放在一起，从新到旧，每行写明来自哪个功能，搜索也覆盖全部记录。原来三个标签不变。
- **个人词典也有搜索框了**（#3869）：Markdown 里的每处匹配用红色粗体标出，按回车、Shift+回车选中下一处、上一处并滚到那里，搜索框旁显示计数，和“在提示词里查找”一样。
- **在答案卡片里为这一次输入选思考强度**（#3879）：回答所用的语言模型服务有思考设置时，翻译和改写的答案卡片在“插入”前面多一个按钮，列出这个服务支持的强度（按设置、关闭、开启、各档 effort，或 1,024、4,096、16,384 token 的预算）。选一个，Voxt 就用同样的输入按这个强度再问一次，替换卡片里的回答。服务的“思考”设置不变，下一次输入照旧用它。
- **听写整理文字时也能选思考强度**（#3940）：听写所用的语言模型服务有思考设置时，模型整理文字期间，录音窗口在“用识别原文”后面多一个“思考”按钮，按钮上写着当前强度（例如“思考：已关闭”），列出的强度和答案卡片相同。选一个，Voxt 就停掉这次请求，用这个强度把同样的话重新整理一遍，只插入这次的结果。服务的“思考”设置不变，下一次输入照旧用它。
- **短输入自动不思考，思考按钮写明会重新问**（#3951）：语言模型服务的思考设为开启、某个强度或预算时，“思考”下面多一个“短输入不思考”开关。少于设定字数（默认 200）的输入按 Voxt 默认的思考发送，服务允许的就关掉思考，短句不用等。更长的输入照旧用选的强度。答案卡片和录音窗口里的思考按钮改成“重新问：思考…”或“重新问：思考（已关闭）”这样的写法，因为选一个强度会停掉这次回答、重新问一遍。

#### 变更

- **“等待语言模型”可以自己填分钟数**（#3941）：语言模型页的这个设置除了自动、1、2、5、10 分钟，多了“自定义…”，选它后旁边出现分钟数输入框。填或点加减到 1 到 60 之间的整数，转录、翻译、改写就至少等这么久，和预设一样。超出 1 到 60 的数不保存。以前存的设置照旧。
- **转录加速改成开关**（#3873）：语音识别模型页里，模型卡片上的“转录加速”现在是一个开关，旁边的 ⓘ 说明它需要一个草稿模型以及草稿有多大。开关决定模型用不用草稿：下载草稿不会再自动打开它，关掉开关时草稿留在磁盘上但不用。开关打开而还没有草稿时，卡片会提示下载。在 2.0.2 或 2.0.3 里下载过草稿的，开关一开始是开着的。
- **语音识别模型在第一次听写前就加载好**：Voxt 启动时、换语音识别模型时就加载转录用的本地模型，不再等到第一次按快捷键。启动后和换模型后的第一次实时文字不用再等加载（36 GB 的 M 系列 Mac 上是 0.2 到 0.5 秒，机器忙或内存小时更久）。模型空闲 10 分钟后照旧从内存卸载。
- **词典很大时听写不再变慢**：听写完成后，Voxt 会把词典里的别名换成对应的词条，现在每个位置只检查以该字开头的别名。词典有 1000 个词条时，4 KB 的转录稿从 57 毫秒降到 2 毫秒，20 KB 的从 280 毫秒降到 8 毫秒（Apple M5 Max）。替换结果不变。
- **翻译选中文字不再等语音识别模型**（#3838）：选中文字后用翻译，只用语言模型（和备用模型），Voxt 不再先打开本地语音识别模型。这个模型空闲 10 分钟后会退出内存，重新加载要等，而翻译选中文字根本用不到它。没装语音识别模型也能翻译选中文字。没选中文字时，翻译照旧录音、用语音识别模型。
- **转录加速更省内存**：语音识别模型的草稿改用 8 位运行，原来是 16 位。在 Voxt 的测试录音上转出的文字逐字相同，语音识别模型少占约 120 MB（0.6B）或 300 MB（1.7B）内存，识别快 1–2%（Apple M5 Max）。
- **历史里很长的记录也能流畅滚动**：打开超过约 5.7 万字的记录详情（一小时的口述、翻译过的长文章）后，每一帧都要把全文重新排版，整个历史页都反应慢：6 万字时每次移动鼠标或滚动要 0.1 秒，10 万字时将近 4 秒。现在打开详情时排版一次，之后每帧 0.3 毫秒（Apple M5 Max）。短一些的记录本来就快。

#### 修复

- **设置引导的翻译、改写两步里，“设置语言模型”按钮能用了**（#3917）：以前按钮在引导后面打开“语言模型”页，看上去没反应。现在引导会先让开，和“模型”一步的同一个按钮一样，页面顶部可以“回到设置引导”的这一步。
- **关掉“在程序坞中显示”后，Voxt 从启动起就不进程序坞**（#3918）：以前 Voxt 先作为程序坞里的 App 启动，启动完才离开程序坞，程序坞图标有时会留下来，比如按 ⌘W 关掉窗口之后。现在 Voxt 启动时就不在程序坞里，只有菜单栏图标。
- **改写能读到网页和 PDF 里选中的文字**（#3835）：在 Chrome 里用 ⌘A 全选一个网页，或在 Acrobat 里选中 PDF 的一页，改写只把你说的指令发给语言模型，模型回答说没有收到文字。原因是这些应用通过辅助功能报告了一个空的选区，Voxt 当成什么都没选，也没有再试复制。现在 Voxt 会在这些地方复制选中的文字（之后把剪贴板还原），和在什么都不报告的应用里一样。在输入框里只有光标、没有选中文字时，仍然不发送文字。
- **不再显示 Voxt 1.x 的公告，也不显示别的语言的公告**：Voxt 读的是 Voxt 1.x 服务器上的公告，那里还挂着 1.13 介绍会议和笔记的中文公告。每个新装 Voxt 2 的用户都会在侧边栏看到这张卡片，英文界面也一样，可 Voxt 2 正式版没有会议和笔记。现在只显示写给 Voxt 2 的公告，中文公告只在中文界面显示。卡片还在的话点 × 关掉，不会再出现。
- **快速模型写长回答时，答案卡片不再跳动**（#3836）：模型出字很快时（比如快速的远程模型写一段长的改写回答），卡片每收到一段文字就往下长几行，一秒跳好几次。现在卡片以稳定的速度逐字显示回答，略落后于模型，每次长四行左右。模型一写完，整段回答马上全部显示，不用等动画。追问的回答也一样。
- **“Beta 更新”能找到 beta 版了**（#3850）：打开 设置 → 诊断与关于 → Beta 更新 后，“检查更新…”和每天的后台检查会提供最新的 beta 版（main 每合入一次改动就构建一个），附更新说明和下载它磁盘映像的“下载”按钮，不用再登录 GitHub 下载。关着开关时和以前一样只提示正式版。Voxt 仍然不会自己安装更新。
- **语音识别模型页的卡片对齐了**（#3854）：单独一张卡片的模型（Parakeet、SenseVoice、Cohere、系统听写等）图标占了一整列，名字、说明、语言和标签比 Whisper、Qwen3-ASR 系列卡片里的靠右，图标下面空着一条。现在图标放在模型名字旁边，文字从卡片边缘开始，和系列卡片一样。
- **关掉答案卡片后不再弹出卡片**：结果插不进去时（没有选中输入框、焦点在密码框，或者粘贴后没有出现），即使转录、翻译的答案卡片关着，Voxt 也会打开卡片。现在只有开着时才打开；关着时 Voxt 把结果复制到剪贴板，浮窗提示按 ⌘V 粘贴。两种情况历史里都有这条结果。改写插不进去时仍然放在卡片里，方便继续追问。
- **OpenAI 和豆包极速版能识别长录音**（#3840）：OpenAI 以及用它的接口的服务一次最多收 25 MB 音频（约 13 分钟），豆包极速版文件识别最多 100 MB（约 52 分钟）。更长的口述，或在历史里点重试，会报“超过 25 MB 上限”。现在 Voxt 把长录音在停顿处切成不超过上限的几段依次发送，再把文字拼起来。
- **长时间口述不再在 10 分钟时被截断**：转录、翻译和改写录到 10 分钟会自己停下，录播客这样的长段口述没按快捷键就结束了。现在录音没有时长上限，录到你按停止为止。
- **会议也按主语言识别**：会议的实时字幕和会后整理的转录稿，现在和听写一样把主语言（设置 → 通用 → 主语言，比如简体中文或繁体中文）告诉语音识别模型。以前会议不告诉模型，由模型自己猜语言。简体还是繁体仍由模型决定，Voxt 不做简繁转换。
- **听写的文字能输入到 Raycast 等启动器的面板里**：Raycast、Tinycast 这类面板接管键盘，但它们的 App 不到前台，以前文字进不去，Voxt 改为弹出结果卡片。现在文字会粘贴到面板的输入框里。
- **会议实时文字停顿后不再重复整句**（开发版）：Voxt 已经显示一句话之后，模型又改了句尾几个字，下一行实时文字会把整句从头再出一遍。现在只显示没变的那部分之后的字。会议结束后生成的转录稿不受影响。
- **系统听写不再丢掉停顿之前说的话**：有些 macOS 版本在短暂停顿后会重新开始识别，结果里只剩停顿之后的话，历史记录和输入的 app 里都一样。现在 Voxt 把每一段都留下，再接起来。
- **按一次 Esc 就能关对话框**（历史设置、设置引导、网络、语言模型的设置）：焦点在带提示的按钮上时（比如对话框右上角的 ×），第一次 Esc 只收起提示，第二次才关掉对话框。
- **日志筛选没有匹配时如实说明**：筛选不到任何一行时，日志页和日志面板显示“没有匹配的记录”和“清除筛选”，不再显示“还没有日志。”
- **语言模型调试页可以按 Esc 或 × 关掉**：从“调试提示词…”打开后，页面上原来没有关闭按钮，只能点侧边栏，或者在还没设置模型时点“打开语言模型页”离开。现在按 Esc 或点右上角的 × 都会回到语言模型页；菜单打开时按 Esc 仍然只关菜单。
- **语音识别模型中途退出，口述也不丢**：录音时本地语音识别模型的进程退出了（崩溃，或内存紧张时被 macOS 结束），停止后以前只得到退出前的实时文字，并带一条警告。现在 Voxt 重新启动模型，识别整段录音，在 M5 Max 上多等约 0.2 秒。
- **浮窗预览里的胶囊留在所选的一侧**（设置 → 浮窗样式）：与屏幕边缘的距离调大时，选“顶部居中”预览里的胶囊画在靠下的位置，选“底部居中”画在靠上的位置。现在距离越大，胶囊越靠近中间，但始终留在自己那一半。
- **在程序坞中显示**（设置 → 通用 → 菜单栏与登录）：Voxt 2.0.0 和 2.0.1 不读 Voxt 1.x 的“在程序坞中显示”，在 Voxt 1.x 里关了它的用户装上 Voxt 2 后仍在程序坞里。没有用 Voxt 2.0.2 或更新的版本导入过 Voxt 1.x 的数据、开关也没关的，下次启动时补读一次；之后再打开，程序坞图标就留着。“在设置中查找”能按名称找到任何一部分里的设置（以前在“通用”里搜“Dock”或“clipboard”什么也找不到），中文界面下也能用英文名称找（搜“Dock”能找到“在程序坞中显示”）。关掉这个开关后，Voxt 窗口仍在最前面。
- **没有 API Key 时测试连接，会用文字说明原因**：在语言模型的对话框里，没保存也没填写 Key 就点“测试连接”，提示改为“请在上方粘贴该服务的 API Key，然后再测试。”旁边的 ⓘ 先给出这句完整的话，再给错误详情。以前 ⓘ 只显示错误代码（`llm.no_key provider="anthropic"`），提示还让你去当前已经在的“语言模型”页。
- **正式版的命令面板不再列出“会议”。** 会议只在开发版里有，在命令面板里选它会打开“未找到”页面。
- **崩溃后有东西可发**：Voxt 在自己的代码里崩溃时，出错时在做什么现在写进 `~/Library/Logs/VoxtNew/crash.log`，下次启动改名为 `crash.log.1`，并在日志里写明。以前这种崩溃不留任何报告（macOS 不为它写），没有东西可以发给我们。「保存诊断包」（设置 → 关于）会带上这两个文件。

## [2.0.2] - 2026-11-09

**How a recording starts.** Pressing the shortcut starts the microphone at once; Voxt does not wait for the speech model. The speech model and the voice detector load in the background while you speak, on the first dictation after Voxt opens and again after ten idle minutes unload them. Everything you say after the microphone has started is recorded and kept in memory until the model is ready, then sent to it: the live text appears later, but the result has all of it, up to the ten-minute limit of one recording. If the overlay says "Starting the microphone…", the microphone has not produced sound yet and words said then are not recorded; while only the icon pulses and the bars follow your voice, the model is still loading and the recording is fine.

#### 新增

- **ChatGPT (Codex)**（“语言模型”页）：在浏览器里用你自己的 ChatGPT 计划登录，就能用来清理、翻译和转写，不用 API Key。登录信息存在这台 Mac 的钥匙串里，它代表你的整个 ChatGPT 账号；退出登录会删除它。计划额度用完时，插入识别结果，“管理用量”打开用量页面；Voxt 不会自己换用备用模型。
- 每个语音识别模型的**转录加速**，在语音模型页它的卡片上：下载一个小的草稿模型（1.7B 模型 726 MB，0.6B 模型 262 MB），这个模型的转录就更快，靠的是推测解码（草稿每步先提出几个词，再由语音识别模型校验）。随时可以删除这个附加组件，模型照常可用。
- **浮窗预览只显示实时语音识别文字**：录音和识别收尾时保留流式内容；整段 Final 和语言模型整理/翻译不在胶囊里回放，结果照常插入或在答案卡片里出现。
- **可编辑完整 system prompt**（转录、翻译、转写 → 你的规则）：在一个文本框里编辑整段 system prompt（默认显示内置文字）；保存后按所写原样发送；恢复默认回到内置；内置分层模式下另有示例用户消息预览（清理与格式）。**设置搜索**：在设置里按 ⌘F 聚焦搜索框，按标题、说明和英文关键词过滤。
- **设置引导：翻译和转写试用步就地配置语言模型**（FB22）：没配语言模型时，这一步会说明缺什么，并提供「设置语言模型」按钮（引导不关闭）。语音模型下载时，模型步和试用步会显示进度；下载中也可以点继续。
- **录音时浮窗胶囊在图标旁显示当前模式**（转录、翻译或转写）。开始录音的快捷键决定模式；录音中按别的功能的快捷键只会停止，和 Voxt 1.x 的 common stop 一样（`hotkey.Route`）。
- **笔记**（新的“笔记”页），和 Voxt 1.x 一样：在任意 app 里单按右 ⌘，说完再按一次，说的内容会存成一条笔记，不会输入到光标处。笔记页按时间从新到旧列出笔记和时间，可以删除。快捷键在笔记页里改（可以关掉，也可以换成别的键）；右 ⌘ 已经被别的快捷键占用时，笔记键一开始是关的，去笔记页选一个。笔记不进历史，清空历史也不会删掉笔记；识别失败或取消的笔记会留在历史里，带着录音，可以重试。
- **快捷键预设**（设置 → 快捷键 → 预设；设置引导里也可选），和 Voxt 1.x 一样：选“右 ⌥”（默认）、“fn”（和 Auralink 相同）、“⌘ 组合”（右 ⌘ 一套）或“鼠标中键 + fn⇧”（中键单击转录、双击转写，fn⇧ 翻译），一次设好转录、翻译、转写和粘贴最近结果的快捷键。你自己改过的快捷键保持不变。选 fn 时，页面会提示 Auralink 和 macOS 的地球键也会响应 fn。
- **鼠标按钮当快捷键**，同 Voxt 1.x：在快捷键的“自定义…”里输入 Mouse3（中键）、Mouse4 或 Mouse5（多数鼠标的两个侧键），最多到 Mouse32，可以单独用，也可以加修饰键（Option+Mouse4）。单按、双击、长按和键盘快捷键一样；这个按钮按下时不再传给前台 App。和 fn、单个修饰键一样，鼠标快捷键需要辅助功能权限。
- **翻译的备用模型**（翻译 → 模型）：语言模型出错时，Voxt 用这里选的模型再翻译一次。历史记录里写的是实际给出译文的模型。
- **按键录入快捷键**，同 Voxt 1.x：转录、翻译、转写、笔记页（以及粘贴最近结果、取消转录）的“自定义…”改成直接按下组合键，比如 ⌥空格，按下就保存；录制时你的快捷键暂停。开了辅助功能时，辅助进程的 **hotkey.record** 还能录 fn、分左右的修饰键和鼠标按钮（只按修饰键也行）；没有辅助功能时应用内录制框仍可用，也可以点“改为手动输入”。快捷键里的 Esc、空格、回车、删除、Tab 写成文字，不再是 ⎋ ↩ ⌫ ⇥；取消转录的下拉菜单里有 Esc。
- **答案卡片的开关**（转录、翻译、转写 → 答案卡片 → 在卡片中显示结果）：三个功能各有自己的开关。转录和翻译默认关，结果直接插入，有选区就替换；转写默认开，和 Voxt 1.x 一样，回答留在卡片里，点“继续”或再按转写快捷键可以追问（转写页上有说明）；关掉后，转写直接替换选中的文字，没有选区就输入到光标处，不能追问。之前在翻译里开着的仍是开的；2.0.3 测试版里那个共用开关开着的，三个都是开的。以前转写的卡片关不掉，转录也没有这个选项。
- **录音中麦克风断开时接着录**，同 Voxt 1.x：说话时拔掉正在用的麦克风，Voxt 换到 设置 → 音频 列表里第一个还连着的麦克风，再没有就用系统默认输入，接着录；浮窗显示一会儿“已切换到 X”。一个输入都没有了才结束录音。
- **隐藏语音模型**（语音模型）：模型卡片上有“隐藏”，隐藏后功能页的语音识别菜单里不再列出它；卡片移到页面底部的“已隐藏的模型”，点“显示”放回去。功能正在用的模型不能隐藏。
- **在一台全新的 Mac 上测试从 Voxt 1.x 更新**：GitHub Actions 工作流（Voxt Upgrade Test）装好 Voxt 1.x，让它从 beta 通道自己更新到新的 Voxt，再检查装好的 app（bundle ID、Developer ID 签名和公证），以及它不用你选文件夹就能导入 Voxt 1.x 的词典。
- **代理的用户名和密码**（语音模型 → 网络… → 自定义），和 Voxt 1.x 一样：自定义代理（HTTP、HTTPS 或 SOCKS5）可以填用户名和密码，两个都填了才使用，模型下载和远程服务的请求都会带上。密码只保存在钥匙串里；它们只给 Voxt 用，共用模型库的其他 app 不会用到。
- **公告**，和 Voxt 1.x 一样：Voxt 启动时和每次切回 Voxt 时（最多 10 分钟一次）读取 Voxt 的公告。新公告显示为侧边栏“设置”上方的一张卡片：点“查看全文”看完整内容，关掉以后不会再出现。读取公告不会发送任何关于你的信息（没有版本号、语言或标识），设置 → 隐私里也写明了。
- **把选中的词加进词典**，和 Voxt 1.x 一样：在任意 app 里选中几个词（最多五个词，或连续最多十个中日韩字），按转录快捷键，Voxt 把它加进词典，不开始录音，浮窗显示“已加入词典”或“词典里已经有”。选中的文字更长或者没有选中文字时照常录音；词典关着时总是录音。
- **词典建议**：Voxt 建议的词显示在词典页的列表上方，写明听成了什么、来自哪里，侧栏显示待处理的条数。“添加”只把词条加入词典，不加别名；“编辑…”可以先改再加入；“忽略”后 90 天内不再出现。
- **词典的用法**（词典页）：页面上有一段可以展开的说明，讲清词条在哪里生效：语音识别提示、语言模型术语表，以及别名在识别文字里的替换。
- **扫描历史，找词条建议**（词典 → 扫描历史…），对应 Voxt 1.x 的一键导入：Voxt 把还没读过的转录结果每 12 条发给转录用的语言模型，把找到的人名和术语放进建议。开始之前对话框写明要把多少条发给哪个模型；词条要你自己点“添加”，词典内容不会发出去。
- **从我的改正中学习**（词典页）：默认关。把转录结果插入别的 App 后，Voxt 会在 30 秒内读取插入处附近的文字；你改了其中某个词，它在词典页建议你改后的写法。只用本地规则，不发送任何文字；读到的文字不保存、不写日志，也不读密码框。
- **菜单栏选麦克风**，Voxt 1.x 有过的功能：菜单栏图标的菜单里有“麦克风”子菜单，列出已连接的输入设备，录音会用的那个打勾。点一个就把它移到 设置 → 音频 列表的第一位，下一次录音用它。设备列表在 Voxt 启动时、每次录音结束后和打开 设置 → 音频 时读取。
- **说结束词停止**（转录 → 快捷键），Voxt 1.x 有过的功能：选 over、end、完毕、好了，或者自己填一个词，最后说出它并停顿一下，录音就像按了快捷键一样停止，这个词不会写进文字。边说边出文字的语音模型（本地 Qwen3-ASR、系统听写、豆包、阿里云百炼、Gemini）才能用，转录、翻译、转写都管用。
- **豆包极速版文件识别**（语音模型 → 豆包 → 模型：`volc.bigasr.auc_turbo`），Voxt 1.x 能用但菜单里没有：用同一组 App ID 和 Access Token，停止录音后把整段录音交给豆包，一次返回结果，不走流式。账号要在火山引擎控制台开通这个资源。超过 100 MB（约 52 分钟）的录音不发送。
- **隐藏不用的功能**（设置 → 通用 → 功能 →“显示的功能”），和 Voxt 1.x 一样：取消勾选翻译、转写或应用增强，它就不出现在侧边栏、⌘K 和首页的统计里，快捷键和场景规则也停用。它的设置会保留，重新勾选就回来。
- **从 Voxt 1.x 导入会带上程序坞显示**（FB17）：设置 → 通用里本来就有“在程序坞中显示”；导入现在读取 1.x 的 `showInDock` 写入 `General.HideDock`，升级后仍可按菜单栏-only 使用。
- **直接用实时文字当结果**（设置 → 音频 → 识别 →“再识别一次整段录音”，默认开）：关掉后，边说边出字的本地语音模型（Qwen3-ASR）停止后不再把整段录音识别一遍，识别完最后几个字，实时文字就是结果，出得更快，准确率略低。实时会话中途断了时，Voxt 仍会识别整段录音。
- **实时文字默认多行显示**（设置 → 浮窗样式 →“实时文字的样式”）：浮窗从开始录音起就是宽框（还没有文字时也是），像输入框一样满行换行，最多长到十行，之后显示最新的几行，开头说的话不会被滚出去。可以像答案卡片一样拖动它，到浮窗消失前都留在拖到的位置。仍可以选“单行”（先横向变宽，再横向滚动）；以前用单行的也会变成多行，因为之前单行就是默认值，没法区分是不是自己选的。
- **答案卡片的大小**（转录、翻译、转写 → 答案卡片 → 卡片大小）：小（和以前一样）、中、大，每个功能各自记住，比如转写的长回答用大卡片，翻译仍用小卡片。每个功能也有自己的“在卡片中显示结果”开关。
- **一键换回 Voxt 1.x 的提示音**（设置 → 音频 → 播放提示音 →“使用 Voxt 1.x 的提示音”）：开始录音时 Pop、停止时 Tink，是 Voxt 1.x 默认的一对。其他提示音照样在选择器里。
- **每家远程服务的识别提示词**（语音模型页 → 某远程服务的设置对话框）：可选文字，接在全局「识别上下文」后面；识别时填入 `{{USER_MAIN_LANGUAGE}}`、`{{USER_OTHER_LANGUAGES}}` 和 `{{DICTIONARY_TERMS}}`。不收提示词的语音服务会忽略它。
- **Voxt 1.x 的其他本地语音识别模型**（语音模型页）：Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (6bit)、Whisper Large v3 Turbo、Whisper Large v3、Whisper Small、Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3、SenseVoice Small、Cohere Transcribe 03-2026、Nemotron 3.5 ASR 0.6B 和 MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize，每个用自己的原生服务运行。Nemotron 和 MOSS 边说边出文字；其他模型在你说话时大约每秒更新一次文字，停止后再识别一遍整段录音。只有 Qwen3-ASR 使用你的词典（卡片上标着“不用词典”）。Qwen3-ASR 和 Whisper 各用一张卡片列出同系列的模型。导入时，Voxt 1.x 下载过的模型直接带过来，不用重新下载（Whisper 连同它的分词器），在 Voxt 1.x 里用着这些模型的功能会继续用它；用 Voxt 2.0.x 导入过的，下次启动时这些下载也会带过来。
- **会议**（开发版）：从“会议”页或 Voxt 菜单开始；可以选仅麦克风、仅系统音频，或两者都录；可以暂停、结束，或确认后取消；历史关闭时可以保存或丢弃；可以让浮窗在屏幕共享中隐藏。会议进行时听写快捷键不响应，“取消”会结束会议。“存储”里列出会议录音，“清除全部”会删掉它们。面向普通用户的入口等 C1-10。
- **会议记录表**（应用里还没有接线）：SQLite 迁移 007 增加 `meetings` 与 `meeting_segments` 表、句子和译文的全文检索，以及 `Store.Meetings()`（创建、更新、追加或按轨道替换句子、列表、删除、未完成与录音到期筛选）。
- **会议权限说明**（会议界面仍在开发版，C1-2 前不对普通用户开放）：设置 → 权限 出现 **系统录音（会议用）**；应用与辅助进程的授权文案说明会议中录制其他 app 的声音，麦克风说明也提到会议。
- **OpenAI 的 Responses API**（语言模型 → OpenAI 或自定义服务 → 高级 → API 格式）：默认仍是 Chat Completions，有的模型只支持 Responses，选它就行。Voxt 会要求 OpenAI 不保存这些请求。从 Voxt 1.x 导入的设置沿用原来的格式。
- **简单的默认语音设置**（设置引导 → 模型）：引导在每台 Mac 上都推荐 Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (8bit)，8 GB 内存的也一样，主按钮是“下载并继续”，不点就什么都不下载；“选择其他模型…”去语音模型页。下载期间按快捷键会提示还在下载，不再退到系统听写。首页一下就能开始下载。
- **其他程序读取听写历史**（设置 → 隐私，默认关闭；[docs/history-access.md](docs/history-access.md)）：只读命令 `Voxt history export`，给你自己的脚本和 agent 用，走只有这个账户能连的 socket（不开网络端口）。每次读取都会记录（不含文字），新程序第一次读会有通知。开关只管这条命令：本账户下没有沙盒的程序仍能直接读取数据库文件。历史设置里还有“导出文字…”，存 Markdown 或 JSONL。
- **新增语言模型调试页**（从命令面板或语言模型页底部进入），对应 1.x 的语言模型调试窗口：选一个已设置的语言模型和一种请求（转录、翻译、转写，或某个应用增强分组），可以在页面里临时修改提示词，改动不会保存；输入文字后点“运行”，页面显示回答、用时和发出的全部消息。点“使用这个提示词”，改动才会存为该功能的规则或分组提示词。这里的提示词就是该功能的“你的规则”，不是另一份（页面上写明）；输入框随提示词变高，只用页面的一个滚动条。

#### 变更

- **“自动”写明现在用哪一个**：功能页的语音模型、语言模型菜单和翻译的目标语言菜单里，“自动”一项写成“自动（现在用：Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (8bit)）”，不再像是下面那个模型的重复项。
- **设置引导接着上次的地方**：再打开时回到离开时的那一步（走完之前），点“设置语言模型”或“选择模型…”离开后，那一页顶部有“回到设置引导”。
- **一次请求必需的权限**：权限分成“必需”（麦克风、辅助功能）和“可选”（语音识别），“依次请求必需的权限”会一个接一个地弹出 macOS 的提示。
- **缺 API Key 的服务会说清楚**：卡片上写“需要 API Key”，带“完成设置”；没有可用的语言模型时，侧边栏的卡片写明是哪个服务，点一下打开它的设置。从 Voxt 1.x 导入的服务马上就显示这个状态。
- **从 Voxt 1.x 导入的结果一眼看清**：分成“已导入”“没有导入”（写明原因）和“还要做”，每件要做的事带“完成设置”；不是在设置引导里导入时，结果显示在一个对话框里。
- “你的规则”和“语音识别提示”各自写明作用阶段：提示给语音模型，规则在识别之后整理文字。
- 设置引导每一步先说明这个功能是什么再让你试：转录照录你说的话，翻译把它译成另一种语言，转写按你的要求写出内容。权限一步写明为什么需要麦克风和辅助功能。
- **语言模型的采样用服务自己的默认值**，和 Voxt 1.x 一样：所有服务上，Voxt 都不再自己发温度（之前清理 0.1、翻译 0.2、转写 0.5）。温度、Top P、Top K、Min P 和几种惩罚项都放进服务对话框里默认折叠的“高级”；留空的输入框写着“服务默认”，什么也不发。之前填过的值保留，照常发送。只接受默认温度的推理模型，不会再在每次听写的第一个请求就拒绝。
- **点一下 ⓘ 就能看说明**：点 ⓘ 显示它的提示，直到点别处或按 Esc 才收起；鼠标停在上面照旧显示。
- **规则框跟着提示词变高，⌘F 可以在里面查找**（转录、翻译、转写 → 你的规则）：输入框随内容增高，大约 36 行以后才在框内滚动。在功能页按 ⌘F 会展开“你的规则”并聚焦“在提示词里查找”：显示有几处（不分大小写），匹配处用强调色标出，按回车跳到下一处。
- **转写能回答关于选中文字的问题**：选中文字后说“解释一下这段”“这是什么意思”，内置提示词会让模型用你说话的语言在卡片里回答，而不是改写原文。你自己写的完整提示词（转写 → 你的规则）照原样发送。
- **清理默认去掉车轱辘话**：清理的示例里加了一句重复的话、只保留一次，所以紧接着又说一遍的同样词句或同一个意思（“今天下班前一定要发，就是说今天下班前一定要发出去”）只留一次；语气词和改口照旧去掉。
- **设置引导画出快捷键在键盘上的位置**：转录、翻译、转写这几步的快捷键下面，画着 Mac 键盘最下面两排，要按的键亮起，比如“右 ⌥”就是空格键右边的 Option 键。
- **宽窗口里页面居中**：全屏或窗口很宽时，首页、转录、翻译、转写、语音模型和语言模型的内容栏放在页面中间，标题和页面按钮跟内容对齐，不再贴着左边、按钮却在最右边。
- **语言模型处理时实时文字留在浮窗里**：停止录音后，浮窗保留说话时显示的文字（转写的指令也是），直到结果输入进去或出现在卡片里，不再只剩一个加载动画。模型写的草稿仍然不在浮窗里回放。
- **转写的回答直接写进卡片**（“在卡片中显示结果”开着时）：模型一开始回答，浮窗就变成满高的答案卡片，回答边写边出现在里面，不再一直转圈等整段写完。点关闭会停止。关掉“显示实时文字”时，和以前一样写完才出卡片。
- **多行实时文字可以往回翻**：实时文字超过浮窗的十行时，浮窗接收滚轮，可以往上翻看前面说的话，新的字不会把它拉回去；滚回底部就重新跟着最新的字。
- **自定义请求参数合成一个 JSON 对象**：语言模型对话框不再有“专家”一节。默认折叠的“高级”最后有一个“请求参数”框，所有自定义请求参数都写在这一个 JSON 对象里，取代原来的额外请求体 JSON 和 Ollama 单独的 Options JSON。它按 JSON merge patch（RFC 7386）最后合并进请求：同名的键以这里为准；值是对象时，与 Voxt 发送的同名对象逐层合并（Ollama 的模型选项写在 `"options"` 里）；值写 `null` 则不发送这个字段。已保存的和从 1.x 导入的 Options JSON 会自动放进 `"options"`。
- **宽窗口里历史、词典、笔记和应用增强也居中**：全屏或窗口很宽时，这几页的列表和面板最宽 1120 点、放在中间，标题和按钮跟着对齐；日志页仍然占满宽度。

#### 修复

- **有辅助功能权限时能录快捷键了**：快捷键“自定义…”的录制框里按 ⌥Q 这类组合键什么都存不上，因为 helper 第一次录制就把自己锁死了；每试一次就多占住 helper 的一个线程，试几次后 helper 可能不再响应（不能录音、不能粘贴），直到重启 Voxt。录制框有焦点时，页面也不再误报缺少辅助功能权限（这时 Voxt 是故意暂停快捷键），“打开权限”也不会一点就消失。
- **“调试提示词…”是一个正常的按钮**：语言模型页底部的这个入口原来拉满整行、文字居中，看不出能点，现在是靠左的按钮；调试页的“打开语言模型”也一样。转写页“答案卡片”的说明改写得更直白。
- **⌘W 关闭窗口。** 开着“关闭窗口后继续运行”（默认开）时，Voxt 留在菜单栏里，快捷键照常可用，和红色 × 一样；只有 ⌘Q 退出。之前窗口菜单里没有“关闭窗口”。
- **在 Voxt 自己的窗口里听写，文字又能输进去了**，和 Voxt 1.x 一样：光标在 Voxt 的输入框里（比如功能页的“你的规则”）时，按快捷键和在别的 app 里一样录音、显示浮窗，文字输入到这个框里。以前在首页和功能页上按快捷键会在页面里开始一次试一试（没有浮窗，结果显示在“试一试”区域），在其他页面上结果进了答案卡片。
- **滚过“你的规则”时页面接着滚**：在页面上开始的滚动，规则框移到指针下面时页面照样往下滚；只有在规则框里开始的滚动才滚规则框，和 macOS 别处一样。长提示词展开时也从开头显示，不再停在结尾。
- **转写能处理长选段**：转写边录音边读选中的文字，读取时限从 1.5 秒放宽到 6 秒（复制兜底 1.5 秒），网页编辑器里的长选段不会再读不到、只按指令写。

- **浏览器输入框（GitHub 等）**：⌘V 已经粘进输入框时，不再误报“文字没有出现在输入位置”。Chromium 类网页编辑器常在粘贴后仍让无障碍读到相同长度；网页区域或 contenteditable 里若对比结果不变，按“无法校验”处理，仍算插入（#3644 FB36）。
- **从 Voxt 1.x 导入的语言模型设置要么能用，要么在结果里说明原因**：没有路径的自定义地址补上 Voxt 1.x 加过的路径，OpenAI 只保留 Voxt 1.x 实际发送的内容，Anthropic 的输出上限保持在思考预算之上，存过 Key 的 Ollama、oMLX、LM Studio 会被要求重新填写，另一台机器上的 http 地址会写明地址规则。
- **从 Voxt 1.x 导入时保持清理关闭，并说明没有导入什么**：Voxt 1.x 里关着转录的清理时导入后仍然关着，填好 Key 后转录的文字不会开始发给语言模型；结果里写明 Codex 和用过它的功能，读不了 Voxt 1.x 的偏好设置时也会说明。`{{SOURCE_TEXT}}` 和 `{{DICTATED_PROMPT}}` 不再原样发给模型。
- **插入时预览立即收起**：结果一开始粘贴进 App，浮窗就淡出，不会再盖在刚插入的文字上停留片刻；插入不了的结果照常显示在答案卡片里。
- **命令面板适应小窗口**：窗口较小、界面缩放调到最大时，命令面板两侧仍留有边距，圆角完整。**应用增强页在宽窗口里用满宽度**，不再限制在 760 点宽。
- **预览超过十行后可以用滚轮翻看**：以前文字实际排版后比预估的长时，浮窗仍让鼠标穿透，滚轮滚动的是下面的 App；现在量出实际高度后，浮窗会接管滚轮。
- **长选区上的追问不再超时**：在答案卡片里追问时，之前的选区和回答会和这次说的话一起重新发给模型。以前等待模型返回第一个字的时限只按这次说的话的长度计算，现在把重发的内容也算进去。
- **转写的卡片随回答自动滚动**：以前长回答会写到卡片可见区域以下，看不到；现在卡片会跟着显示最新生成的内容，你向上滚动阅读时不会被拉回去。
- **在卡片上直接调整大小**：卡片右下角新增一个小按钮，依次切换小、中、大三档（大之后回到小），和功能页上的“卡片大小”是同一个设置。屏幕较矮时，中、大两档的正文高度会缩小到屏幕放得下为止，卡片不再超出屏幕。
- **“你的规则”不再套一层滚动条**：规则框的高度随提示词的长度增长，长提示词跟着整个页面滚动，页面上只有一个滚动条。打字或移动光标时，页面会跟着光标滚动；查找时，页面会滚到匹配的位置。
- **英文文案过了一遍**：约 45 条有翻译腔的英文改成了地道的说法，例如设置引导里的 “try” 改成 “test”，“Voxt may not use the microphone” 改成 “Voxt does not have microphone access”，“the app in front” 改成 “frontmost app”，“Scene prompt” 改成 “Group prompt”，“Cancel transcription” 改成 “Cancel recording”（它也取消翻译和转写），“the previous Voxt” 改成 “Voxt 1.x”。英文统一用英式拼写，原来剩下的三处美式拼写也已改掉。中文文案只改了两处，与英文保持一致：“场景提示词”改为“分组提示词”，“取消转录”改为“取消录音”。
- **答案卡片随长回答变大，并且可以拖动**：卡片的高度跟着内容增长，直到屏幕放不下才在卡片里滚动，转写边写边长也一样；卡片大小只决定宽度。按住卡片的空白处或标题栏，可以把卡片拖开：卡片变长时保持在拖到的位置，不会超出屏幕；关掉后，下一张卡片回到浮窗设置的位置。
- **模型出第一个字慢，翻译和转写不再报错**：语言模型的第一个字不再单独限时（原来是 15 秒）。推理模型要先思考、Ollama 第一次要加载模型，都会有一段时间不出字，翻译和转写就会报“语言模型没有及时响应”（转录则悄悄退回识别原文）。整个请求的总时限，以及回答开始流出后两个字之间最多 5 秒的限制，都保持不变。

### Added

- Voxt 2.0.0, rebuilt from scratch: voice input for macOS on local models only. Press a shortcut in any app, speak, and the text appears where your cursor is. Audio and text never leave your Mac.

- Local language models: a balanced one (recommended), a fast one and a translation model, for clean-up, translation and rewriting.

- Dictionary: its terms help the speech model hear your names and jargon, and the language model write them as you spell them.
- The Local models page: download, pause and resume, every file verified, delete; the memory a loaded model uses. Downloads use the system's proxy, or a mirror when Hugging Face cannot be reached.

- **Keep models loaded** (Local models → Advanced): how long a model stays in memory unused. Automatic, the default, is as before (speech 10 minutes, language models 5); you can choose 5, 15 or 30 minutes, 1 hour, or until Voxt quits, so that every dictation starts at once. Other apps that use this model library can still ask Voxt to free the memory of models it is not using. A change applies at once.
- **Sounds to choose** (Settings → Audio → Play sounds): the start and the stop sound are each chosen from a sound library (from React Bits Sound Lab, in the official builds; Tally and Wrap by default), or macOS's system sounds, or none. A compact picker searches them (names and tags), filters by type, plays each with ▶ (again to stop) and sets their volume in steps; a Try button plays the choice. A Voxt pair chosen in an earlier build is kept as those system sounds. A build from source without the library plays Pop and Tink.
- **Mute system audio while recording** (Settings → Audio, on by default): the current output device is muted from just after the start sound until the recording stops, so that videos, music or calls are not recorded; a device you muted stays muted, one you unmute during the recording stays on, and a device that cannot be muted leaves the recording as it is (the overlay says so). No permission is needed. If Voxt or its helper stops while muting, the sound comes back at once, or at the next start.
- **A notice when your Mac is short of memory**: when the language model was slow, or did not answer in time, while the Mac's swap was over 90% full, the overlay says "Low memory slows the model: quit unused apps." after the text went in. It shows at most once each time Voxt runs.
- **History settings** (the button beside History's search field): keep the recordings (the same switch as Settings → Privacy), choose the folder they are kept in (the recordings already kept move there, each one copied and checked before the original is deleted; if the folder is on a drive that is not connected, new recordings go to the default folder until it is back), see how much room they take, export them (all, or those of the tab shown) into a folder you choose, named by their date and first words, or delete them all and keep the texts. **Automatic clean-up**, on as in Voxt: entries older than 3 months are deleted with their recordings; you can choose 1 week to 1 year, keep at most 50 to 1000 entries of each feature, or turn it off. A new setting that would delete entries now asks first, with their number.

The entry is updated in place (text, status, time); nothing is pasted. The overlay's notice of a failed recognition opens the entry and retries it.
- **Long dictations are recognised while you speak**: after a minute, finished stretches are recognised in the background, cut at pauses, so Stop leaves only the last half-minute or so (on a busy Mac, 104 s of speech: 26 s → 2.4 s after Stop).
- **The overlay says what a recording is waiting for.** When the microphone is slow to start (the first dictation after Voxt starts on a Mac short of memory), the microphone icon pulses inside a ring and the overlay says "Starting the microphone…": what you say before the bars light up is not recorded. While the speech model is still loading (the first dictation, or after it was unloaded), only the icon pulses and the bars keep showing your voice: the recording works, and the live text comes once the model is ready.

### Changed

- **Voxt 2.0.0 replaces the Swift Voxt 1.x.** Installing it replaces Voxt.app in Applications. The settings, dictionary and history of Voxt 1.x stay in its own folder and are not brought over, and Voxt asks for its permissions again (the setup guide walks you through them).
- **Models are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/ActNow/models**, shared with other apps that use this model library, instead of the Hugging Face cache (~/.cache/huggingface), which cleaning tools treat as a cache. Models already there are brought over at the first start without downloading them again (hard links: the cache stays as it is). Time Machine leaves models out; Settings → Storage → "Back up models with Time Machine" turns it on for all of them.

The terms that a dictation mentions now go with the dictation instead of changing the model's instructions each time.
- The fast speech model gets the 30 most recently changed terms of your dictionary. A longer list made it slower and less accurate; the accurate model still gets up to 500.
- **The result goes where you are typing when it is ready.** If you switch to another app during or after the recording and click into a text field there, the text is inserted there; the app you started in is not brought back. ### Fixed

- A result that was pasted but did not show up (in a web page in Arc) is no longer lost without a word. Voxt now checks that the focused text field changed after the paste. When it did not, the result waits in the answer card and stays on the clipboard (press ⌘V), the auto key is not pressed, and the card says so; when another app had secure input on (a password field, a terminal's Secure Keyboard Entry), the card says that. Where the field cannot tell (some web editors), the text is pasted as before. Your clipboard now comes back as soon as the text shows, instead of half a second later.
- A long dictation no longer fails after 30 seconds of recognition and loses everything (a 4m51s dictation on a Mac short of memory). Recognition now gets 60 s plus 1 s per second of recording; a model that stops answering is still caught after 15 s. If full recognition fails anyway, the live text is used (cleaned up and inserted as usual) and the overlay says so.
- The speech model uses much less memory on long recordings: under 4 GB for a 5-minute dictation (Accurate model) instead of up to 10 GB, which pushed your other apps into swap and slowed the Mac. Recognising a recording again is faster, too.
- When recognition fails or finds no text, the entry keeps the recording for 7 days even when Keep the recordings is off, so that you can retry it; a successful retry deletes it.
- Dictionary terms no longer appear at the end of a dictation where you did not say them ("… 嗯。Voxt, Omni, SGLang."). The speech model wrote out the dictionary it was given when part of a recording had no speech, such as the pause before Stop at the end of a long dictation. Such an ending is now checked against the same audio recognised without the dictionary, and terms you did not say are removed; terms you did say stay, also at the end of a sentence.
- Voxt no longer crashes now and then while it checks its Microphone and Accessibility permissions (when the helper starts or restarts). The check could finish on a different system thread from the one it started on.
- The global shortcut tap is no longer reported ready when macOS created it but stripped its keyboard events (Accessibility granted without Input Monitoring): the granted events are read back and verified, a partial tap is refused and retried every 2 s, plain shortcuts keep working through the fallback, and the Shortcuts settings show the permission notice.
- Chinese dictation is no longer turned into English on a Mac whose first language is English. Without a main language set in Settings → General, the speech model was told to expect the interface's language (English there) and the Fast model translated most Chinese recordings; now it detects the language itself unless you set a main language or use Voxt in Chinese.
- Translating what you say no longer removes 额 and 唔 from inside words first: 金额 (amount) became 金, and Cantonese 唔 (not) was dropped, which reversed the meaning. They are now removed only as a hesitation that opens a clause, before a pause.
- A translation or rewrite that stopped at the language model's length limit is no longer inserted, nor put over your selection: it shows in the answer card, which says it may be incomplete.
- **Clear the history** (Settings → Privacy) now also removes the cleared texts from the database file. Before, they stayed in its free space until it was reused, readable by anyone who opened the file.
- A long recording recognised in parts (Transcribe again and Retry in History) no longer loses the space where two parts meet ("the Republic.Socrates begins"); Chinese still gets none.
- A dictation of over a minute no longer ends with a word you did not say ("Okay.") when you pause before you stop: the pause at the end is no longer cut off and recognised on its own.

- 粘贴了却没有出现的结果（Arc 里的网页）不会再悄无声息地丢掉。Voxt 现在会确认粘贴后焦点所在的输入框确实变了。没有变时，结果留在答案卡片里，并且留在剪贴板上（按 ⌘V 即可粘贴），不按自动按键，卡片里写明原因；另一个 app 开启了安全输入（密码框、终端的“安全键盘输入”）时，卡片会说明这一点。输入框无法告知内容的地方（部分网页编辑器）照常粘贴。原来的剪贴板在文字出现后立即恢复，不再固定等半秒。
- 长口述不会再因为识别超过 30 秒而失败、什么都没留下（内存紧张的 Mac 上一段 4 分 51 秒的口述）。识别时限改为 60 秒加录音每秒 1 秒；模型停止响应仍在 15 秒内发现。完整识别仍然失败时，使用实时识别的文字（照常整理、插入），浮窗会说明。
- 长录音时语音识别模型占用的内存大大减少：5 分钟的口述（精准模型）不到 4 GB，以前最多 10 GB，会把其他应用挤进 swap、拖慢 Mac。重新识别一段录音也更快了。
- 识别失败或没有识别出文字时，即使“保存录音”关着，这条记录也保留录音 7 天，方便重试；重试成功后删除。
- 词典里的词不会再出现在你没说过它们的口述末尾（“……嗯。Voxt，Omni，SGLang。”）。录音中没有说话的部分（比如长口述结束前、按停止之前的停顿）会让语音模型把给它的词典原样写出来。现在这样的结尾会和不带词典识别的同一段音频核对，删掉你没说过的词条；说过的词条照常保留，在句末也一样。
- Voxt 检查麦克风和辅助功能权限时（辅助进程启动或重启时）不会再偶尔崩溃。原因是这次检查可能在另一个系统线程上结束，而不是开始时的那个线程。
- 全局快捷键的 event tap 不再在 macOS 创建了它却剥掉键盘事件时误报就绪（只给了辅助功能、没给输入监控）：现在会回读并校验实际授予的事件，不完整的 tap 会被拒绝并每 2 秒重试；普通快捷键继续走备用通道，快捷键设置页会显示权限提示。
- 系统首选语言是英文的 Mac 上，中文口述不会再被转成英文。以前没在 设置 → 通用 里设主语言时，语音模型会被告知按界面语言（那里是英文）识别，快速模型把大部分中文录音翻成了英文；现在除非你设了主语言或者用中文界面，否则由模型自己判断语言。
- 口述翻译不会再先把词里的“额”“唔”去掉：“金额”变成了“金”，粤语的“唔”（不）被去掉、意思反了。现在只有分句开头、后面有停顿的“额”“唔”才当作语气词去掉。
- 翻译或改写的回答到了语言模型的长度上限就停了时，不再插入，也不再替换你选中的文字：它显示在答案卡片里，并提示可能不完整。
- **清空历史**（设置 → 隐私）现在也会把清掉的文字从数据库文件里抹掉。以前它们留在文件的空闲空间里，直到被覆盖，打开文件的人都能读到。
- 分段识别的长录音（历史里的“重新识别”和“重试”）在两段相接的地方不会再丢掉空格（“the Republic.Socrates begins”）；中文照旧不加空格。
- 一分钟以上的口述，停止前停顿一下时，结尾不会再多出一个没说过的词（“Okay.”）：结尾的停顿不再被单独切出来识别。
