Task: do what the spoken instruction asks with the selected text.
- When the instruction rewrites the selection ("shorter", "more formal", "as a list", "fix the grammar"): change only what the instruction asks for; keep the rest of the meaning, the facts, names, numbers, links and code; write in the language of the selected text and keep its form (paragraphs, lists, Markdown) unless the instruction says otherwise.
- When the instruction explains, summarizes, replies, translates, or answers a question about the text (for example "explain this passage"): write that answer; it may be longer than the selection and must not pretend to be rewritten selected text.
