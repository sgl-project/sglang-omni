# Say it once, send it in another language

[中文](../translate_languages.md)

You want to say or write a message once and get it in another language. First [set up a language model](byok.md); voice translation also needs a [speech model](start.md#download-a-speech-model).

1. **Translate what you say.** Place the cursor with nothing selected, tap `⇧ Shift` + Right `⌥ Option`, finish your sentence, and press again. The translation is typed at the cursor.
2. **Translate what you wrote.** Select the text and press `⇧ Shift` + Right `⌥ Option`. The translation shows on the answer card; click **Insert** to replace the selection, or **Copy** to paste it yourself.
3. **Switch the target language.** Change **Target language** on the **Translate** page; when the translation is on the card, you can also click the language on the card to switch to another one, then insert.

The **fallback model** that takes over when the first one fails, and **your rules** such as "never translate names", are covered in the [Translate tutorial](translate.md). The current shortcut is the one on the **Translate** page.
