# Write a message by voice

[中文](../write_by_voice.md)

You want to write a chat message or a note faster than typing. First [download a speech model](start.md#download-a-speech-model).

1. Click into the input field to place the cursor. Tap Right `⌥ Option`, say what you want to write at a natural pace, and tap Right `⌥ Option` again.
2. The text appears at the cursor. When a [language model](byok.md) is chosen on the **Transcribe** page, Voxt removes fillers and fixes punctuation before typing.
3. When a name or term keeps coming out wrong, add it to the [dictionary](names_right.md) once and it comes out right next time.

<div class="vx-anim" data-scene="dictate" data-lang="en"></div>

Press `Esc` to cancel at any time. Re-pasting the last result and retrying a failed recognition are covered in the [Transcribe tutorial](transcribe.md). The current shortcut is the one on the **Transcribe** page.
