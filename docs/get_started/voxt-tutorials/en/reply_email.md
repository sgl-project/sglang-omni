# Read and answer an email in another language

[中文](../reply_email.md)

An English email lands in your inbox. You want to understand it and answer in English without typing. First [set up a language model](byok.md) and [download a speech model](start.md#download-a-speech-model).

1. **Understand it.** In your mail app, select the body, tap `⌃ Control` + Right `⌥ Option`, say "what does this email say, and what does it need from me", and press the shortcut again. The answer card explains it in your language.
2. **Dictate the reply.** Click into the reply field with nothing selected, tap `⇧ Shift` + Right `⌥ Option`, say your reply in your own language, and press again. The English translation is typed at the cursor.
3. **Polish it.** Select the English you just wrote, press `⌃ Control` + Right `⌥ Option`, say "more polite", and click **Insert** on the answer card to replace the selection. To refine further, click **Continue** and say one more instruction.

Step 1 is [Rewrite](rewrite.md): with text selected, anything you say is answered against the selection. Step 2 is [Translate](translate.md). The current shortcuts are the ones on the **Rewrite** and **Translate** pages.
