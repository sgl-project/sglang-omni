# Polish a draft without rewriting it

[中文](../polish_draft.md)

You wrote a paragraph and want it shorter, clearer or more formal without rewriting it yourself. First [set up a language model](byok.md) and [download a speech model](start.md#download-a-speech-model).

1. Select the text, tap `⌃ Control` + Right `⌥ Option`, say what you want — "half the length, more formal" — and press the shortcut again.
2. The answer card shows the rewrite. Click **Insert** to replace the selection, or **Copy** to paste it yourself.
3. Not there yet? Click **Continue** and say one more thing, like "shorter still", and the card keeps refining.

<div class="vx-anim" data-scene="rewrite-card" data-lang="en"></div>

With text selected you can also ask questions about it ("does this repeat itself"); with nothing selected, say what to write and Voxt writes new text. More in the [Rewrite tutorial](rewrite.md). The current shortcut is the one on the **Rewrite** page.
